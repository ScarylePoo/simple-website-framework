Where things live

  • Pages are files in pages/. The address of a page is its file name: pages/about.html is /about.
  • Posts are files in pages/posts/. They appear on the archives page once they have a title, date, image and excerpt.
  • The menu is pages/navigation.html. Add a link there for each page you create.
  • The footer columns are pages/footer/footer1.html, footer2.html and footer3.html.
  • The look comes from the theme named in config/config.php. Copy themes/starter to a new folder to make your own.

The full documentation is at simplewebframe.work.
